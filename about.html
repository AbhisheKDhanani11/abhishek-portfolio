How it all started

My journey into the world of technology began during my undergraduate studies at Government Engineering College in Gandhinagar, India. While I was initially exploring the broad landscape of Computer Science—wrestling with software engineering principles and database management—I quickly realized that my true fascination lay in the logic of algorithms and advanced mathematics.

The turning point happened when I started working with raw data. The idea that you could take thousands of chaotic data points and extract a clear, actionable narrative felt like a superpower. That curiosity led me to dive headfirst into Python, Pandas, and Scikit-learn, shifting my focus entirely toward the analytical side of tech.

Where I Am Now

Fast forward to today, I have crossed the globe and am currently based in Connecticut, thriving in my Master's program in Computer Science (Data Science track) at Sacred Heart University. My focus has evolved from basic data analysis to building complex, intelligent systems.

Whether it's tinkering with Hugging Face Transformers to detect fake news, building RAG systems for document summarization, or engineering API pipelines, my goal is to bridge the gap between theoretical machine learning and practical, real-world applications.
